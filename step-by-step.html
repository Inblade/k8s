About the project

ЗАМЕНИТЬ текст Step by Step is an international watercolour art project for artists from around the world. It offers an opportunity to look at the art of watercolour from the inside and to reveal the stages of creating a watercolour work of any complexity.

ЗАМЕНИТЬ текст The project brings together festivals, exhibitions, plein-airs and step-by-step tutorials in watercolour and graphics.
