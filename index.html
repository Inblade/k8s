About me Step by Step project

Hi, I’m Julia

I am an artist, and this is the main thing you need to know about me. Living and creating in harmony with the surrounding world is my lifestyle.

I love nature very much, traveling and walking, for one hour or one year — it doesn’t matter. The world is beautiful and my goal is to explore it using all the possibilities, without violating the usual rules of my life. This site is about watercolours, various directions in art and about the travels. International watercolour projects, exhibitions, plein-airs. Watercolour and graphics step by step, the secrets of craftsmanship, with art in the heart.
